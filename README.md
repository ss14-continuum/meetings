# SS14 Continuance Commission Records

This repository contains the records of the SS14 Continuance Commission or CC
for short. We are group of SS14 forks that formed to promote collaboration and
ensure SS14 will continue to persist long into the future. Our meetings are
currently redistricted to project management and senior maintainers of our
members servers (with a few exceptions) however we will be publishing our
meeting minutes at this location for public viewing. Some items may be redacted
in the public version in the event sensitive topics were discussed.

Moving forward, we intend to meet every two weeks from the second meeting until
father notice or lack of interest among members.

### Joining

If you are a ss14 server lead that wishes to join you can contact one of the following:
- aquif - @dffdff2423 on discord (551544963201105931) and github.
- Chief_Engineer - am.ghost on discord (267392122179682307) and @Chief-Engineer on github
- mqole - mqole on discord (493234058088677379) and @mqole on github
- Kazne - kazne on discord (132690872616747008) and @AftrLite on github

You will be asked to provide a brief overview on why your server wishes to join
and you request is put to a vote among the group. If you are rejected we will
provide the general consensus as to why. As a general rule, we are looking for
servers that have not had any recent drama and have a welcoming community.

If you are unaffiliated with a specific but are a major figure in the ss14
community (particularly on the development side) you can join as a non-voting
unaffiliated member. The process will work the same as above. "Major figure" is
deliberately left vague. The current unaffiliated members include content
creators and major ss14 contributors.
